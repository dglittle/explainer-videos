v0.2 (Oct-19). Venn: red and green additive circles slide together. They first touch at 2.65 s, which the render will line up with your “yellow”. Split: the overlap (a lens of pure rgb(255,255,0)) stays put while the red and green parts slide away, each with a bite missing. Zoom: as in v0.1, the frame is drawn at the video's own 1920×1080. The pixel grid you zoom into is that same frame, every video pixel blown up 70× and split into its red, green and (off) blue sub-pixels, so the pixels you see are the video's real ones. Diamond: each point of the diamond is rgb(255·r, 255·g, 0), with r and g running 0→1 along the edges. That puts black at the bottom, red on the left, green on the right and yellow at the top. It starts under fog, and the three shapes morph into its red, yellow and green corners. Brightness: the lines are r+g = constant in plain screen values, so they come out horizontal. The straight red→green path runs along the r+g = 1 line, and its middle is rgb(128,128,0), the same olive as v0.1's ending.
