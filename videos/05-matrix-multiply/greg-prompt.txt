(during this whole time, a cool animation of the matrix multiplication trick we're going to tell about in a moment). This is a nice way for humans to multiply matricies on paper. We have our first matrix. And then, rather than drawing our next matrix next to it, we instead draw it above. And then, this implied space is the result. and to calculate each cell, we need only trace our finger left to get the stuff we care about in the first matrix, and up to get the stuff we care about in the second matrix. And then just do the regular multiply all the elements and sum them. happy manual matrix multiplicating!" thanks!!
